Project Snapshot (Palo Alto, Ramona St area)
Matched the existing fence across the yard: same height, step-down pattern, rail/picket layout, and trim profile

Posts set 24" deep, laser-plumb and braced while curing

Gate latch tuned so it clicks shut cleanly—no wrestling needed

Two-day build, tidy cleanup, zero surprises

—

How we built it (quick timeline)

Day 1 – Layout & Structure
Marked utilities with 811/PG&E (always before we dig)

Pulled a few old concrete piles and cleared plants in the fence line

Snapped a string line for a dead-true run, drilled post holes, set pressure-treated 4x4s in fast-set concrete

Braced every post in two directions to keep them perfectly plumb while curing

Day 2 – Rails, Pickets & Finish
Installed kickboards, bottom rail, pickets, top rail, and trim to mirror the older fence board-for-board

Dialed in the step-downs to match the grade and the twin fence across the yard

Tuned the front gate latch so it closes as the gate swings—no extra push needed

Final clean, material haul-away, and a walkthrough with Leslie

—

Quality details we obsess over
Plumb on four axes: up/down, left/right, front/back, and post rotation

Drainage at the post base so water sheds away—less rot, longer life

Tight picket alignment to prevent waves and gaps as the wood seasons

Hidden or clean-lined fasteners for a tidy face and easy future maintenance

Problem-solving at the stone pilaster near the street: we planned around the existing footing and blended the join so it looks intentional and clean
